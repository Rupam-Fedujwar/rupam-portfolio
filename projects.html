A Digital Twin for Efficient Movement of Pilgrims in Uttarakhand, India

July 2022 -- Present

Developing a comprehensive digital twin solution to optimize the movement and management of pilgrims during religious events in Uttarakhand. This project leverages IoT sensors, real-time data analytics, and simulation models to enhance crowd management and safety.

Key Features:

  • Real-time crowd monitoring and analysis
  • Predictive modeling for crowd flow
  • Integration with transportation management systems
  • Emergency response optimization

Technologies:

IoT Digital Twin MATSim Python GIS

QGIS Web-GIS Mobile Development Spatial Analysis

Traffic Engineering VISSIM Statistical Analysis R
